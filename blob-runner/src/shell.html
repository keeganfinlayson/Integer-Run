A narrow escape.

Read the circles and look ahead. Steer as swarms approach, become positive to pass the blue stone gate, then reach the single gate with 0 < |value| < 6.

Blue is positive. Red is negative. A blue stone gate admits positive armies. Same signs join; opposite signs cancel. Zero ends your run.

Steer with ← →, A/D, or drag across the road.
How to play

Look ahead. Then steer.

  • Use arrows, A/D, bottom arrows, or drag left/right. At operation circles, the nearest lane is selected.
  • Operation circles change your exact value. Negative multiplication reverses color.
  • Both incoming swarms move toward your group. Front contact determines which interacts first; the other keeps approaching.
  • Same signs join. Opposite signs cancel at their point of front contact; equal quantities disappear together.
  • If your army reaches zero at any point, you lose, even if the opposing army has survivors. The blue stone gate spans the road and admits positive armies only. Choose the sign you need using the operation circles before it.
  • The final gate requires |value| < 6. The final lanes rejoin automatically. The entire swarm either passes or stops.
  • Space pauses; R restarts. Escape closes Help. Fullscreen depends on browser support.
