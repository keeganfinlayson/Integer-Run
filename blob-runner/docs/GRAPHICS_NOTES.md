# Graphics — build 10

Retains the restored glossy nonhuman blobs, textured wooden bridge, elevated
view, and lateral camera tracking. Adds planted faceted islets rooted at the
water plane, tree silhouettes, multi-face stone posts, and three course palettes:
river green/blue, warm amber, and cool twilight. Raised parapets, deck thickness,
shaded masonry, and near/far parallax continue to provide depth.

The sign gates now carry explicit POSITIVE + and NEGATIVE − labels. Army
quantities remain outside all army bounds, with the player's number below its
formation. There is no top "Your swarm" panel. Operation discs stay on the ground.

Creature variants, crowd mass textures, plank grain, stone posts, background,
and each theme's islet sprite are generated once and cached. Per-frame geometry
and sprite budgets remain bounded. Canvas 2D, no individual physics, remote
artwork, 3D engine, or shaders. All artwork is original source in src/art.js.
