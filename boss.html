Dr. Fin Arena

Try the boss without playing a course. The same forward perspective opens into a wider arena.

Absorb → change sign → counter. Blue (positive) blobs add to matching blue armies; red (negative) blobs add to matching red armies. Opposite colors cancel. Approach with the opposite sign to cancel its body continuously. Both become smaller together. Retreat before your army reaches zero.

There are three small glowing spots on the floor: one positive addition, one negative subtraction, and one multiplication. Each operation stays the same until used; it does not change to target your army. All three are usually safe when they appear, and at least two remain safe. Reaching or crossing zero with addition or subtraction is a loss. Negative operations glow red; positive-only operations glow blue.

Dr. Fin starts at +100. Watch the warning, then avoid or use the ×(−1) sign wave. Four separate groups enter from different places around the edge of the arena: two blue positive groups and two red negative groups, each with a different integer value. They travel along different paths and retain their signs. Opposite signs reduce his health; matching signs heal him. A zero army is eliminated.

Grow, flip, counter

Arrow keys/WASD or tap/drag anywhere to move. Absorb the boss’s matching-color blobs. Use a ground-light operation to get the opposite sign, then approach the boss. Opposite signs shrink together; matching signs transfer your blobs to the boss, making him larger. Back out before your army reaches zero.

Three operation lights appear on the stone floor: a positive addition, a negative subtraction, and a multiplication. Their numbers are fixed while visible, even after an army encounter or sign wave. They are chosen to be useful, not intentionally lethal; changes in your army can still make an operation dangerous. You lose if addition or subtraction reaches or crosses zero—for example, −8 + 16 is a loss. A used light fades as a new one materializes elsewhere. All three remain available, with at least two safe operations. Each ×(−1) wave flips your sign without changing its magnitude; incoming blobs retain their original sign.

Operations are written in bright glowing light directly on the ground; no outcome previews or physical pads. Used spots fade and new spots appear elsewhere. Each attack brings four independently moving groups: two positive blue and two negative red with different integer values. Work with opposite signs to reduce Dr. Fin from 100 to zero.
